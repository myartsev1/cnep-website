Basic science, cutting-edge engineering and translational neuroscience.

At our most recent gathering on January 16th, 2026, over 100 participants came together to present and discuss work spanning basic science, cutting-edge engineering, and translational neuroscience.

The meeting highlighted both the breadth of ongoing research and the collaborative spirit that defines CNEP, providing a forum for open exchange, new connections, and shaping the future direction of the center.

CNEPCenter for Neural Engineering & Prostheses

The Center for Neural Engineering & Prostheses is a collaborative enterprise of UCSF and the University of California, Berkeley, developing technology to restore function for people with neurological disabilities.
